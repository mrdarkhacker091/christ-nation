Admission Requirements

Parents or guardians should prepare the following documents before beginning the admission process:

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport Photograph of the Child
  • Previous Academic Reports (if applicable)

Additional Documents

  • Transfer Letter (for transfer students)
  • Medical Information (if necessary)
  • Parent or Guardian Contact Information

All information provided during the admission process must be accurate and complete.

Assessment and Placement

Depending on the class being applied for, applicants may be invited for:

  • Entrance Assessment
  • Interview Session
  • Academic Placement Test

The purpose of the assessment is to determine the appropriate class placement and identify areas where support may be needed.

Admission Decision

After the review of all submitted information and assessment results, successful applicants will receive an admission offer from the school. Parents will be contacted using the information provided during the application process.

Enrollment

Upon acceptance, parents will be required to:

  • Confirm acceptance of admission
  • Complete enrollment documentation
  • Pay applicable fees
  • Receive student onboarding information

Enrollment is only considered complete after all required procedures have been successfully completed.

Important Notice

Submission of an admission application does not automatically guarantee admission. All applications are reviewed according to the school's admission policies, available spaces, and academic requirements.
