Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

We understand that choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you will make. To help you, we have compiled answers to the most frequently asked questions about Christ Nation Group of Schools.

If you cannot find the answer you are looking for, please do not hesitate to contact us directly — we are always happy to help.

Admissions

How do I apply for admission?
You can apply by completing the online application form or visiting the school admissions office at any of our campuses. Our admissions team will guide you through the process and answer any questions you may have.
What documents are required for admission?
Required documents typically include:
  • Birth certificate (original and photocopy)
  • Recent passport photographs (4 copies)
  • Previous school records / academic transcripts
  • Medical records / immunization card
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Completed application form
  • LIN (Learning Identification Number) – if available
Is there an entrance examination?
Yes, prospective students may be required to take an entrance assessment before admission is granted. The assessment is designed to evaluate the child's academic readiness and identify areas where additional support may be needed.
When does the admission process begin?
Admissions are open throughout the year, subject to available spaces. However, we recommend applying early as spaces are limited and fill up quickly. The main admission intake is at the beginning of each academic session.
Do you accept transfer students?
Yes, transfer students are welcome, subject to available spaces and successful completion of the admission process. Transfers are accepted at the beginning of each term. Please contact the admissions office for specific requirements.
Can parents visit the school before enrollment?
Yes, we welcome prospective parents to schedule a campus tour and meet our staff. This is an excellent opportunity to see our facilities, observe classes in session, and ask any questions you may have.

Academics

What curriculum does the school offer?
Our teaching and learning programmes are guided by the New Revised Basic Education Curriculum, developed in line with the standards and directives of the Federal Ministry of Education and the Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC).
What are the school hours?
School hours are from 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Our doors open at 7:00 AM for early drop-off. Parents are kindly requested to ensure timely pick-up after school hours.
What is the average class size?
Our average class size is between 15 and 25 students, ensuring personalized attention and support for every child. This allows our teachers to focus on each student's individual needs and learning pace.

Do you offer Abacus / Mental Mathematics?
Yes! We offer Abacus and Mental Mathematics for all students in:
  • Nursery — building early number sense and concentration
  • Primary — strengthening calculation speed and accuracy
  • Junior Secondary (JSS 1-3) — enhancing mental arithmetic and problem-solving skills
The Abacus programme helps students develop strong mental calculation abilities, improves concentration, and builds confidence in mathematics. It is a proven method that makes learning mathematics fun and engaging.
Do you prepare students for national examinations?
Yes, we prepare students for all relevant national examinations, including:
  • Common Entrance (Primary 6)
  • BECE (Basic Education Certificate Examination — JSS 3)
  • WAEC (West African Senior School Certificate Examination)
  • NECO (National Examination Council)
  • JAMB (Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board — UTME)
Our students consistently achieve excellent results, with a 95%+ pass rate in WAEC and NECO examinations.
Are qualified teachers available in all subjects?
Yes, all subjects are taught by qualified and experienced teachers committed to academic excellence. Our teachers hold relevant degrees and certifications, and they participate in ongoing professional development to stay current with best practices.
Do students have access to computer and science laboratories?
Yes, students have access to well-equipped ICT laboratories and science laboratories for practical learning. These facilities enable students to engage in hands-on experiments, coding, research, and technology projects that reinforce classroom learning.

Fees and Payments

How much are the school fees?
School fees vary by class level and campus. Please contact the admissions office for the latest fee schedule. We provide a transparent breakdown of all fees, including tuition, uniforms, and extracurricular activities.
Are payment plans available?
Yes, we offer flexible payment options for parents, including termly and instalment plans. Please speak with our bursary office to discuss a payment arrangement that works for your family.

Yes, we offer a wide range of extracurricular activities, including:

  • Debating and Public Speaking
  • Chess Club

Is transportation available?
Yes, school bus services are available in selected areas. Our buses are safe, well-maintained, and supervised by trained attendants. Please contact the school office to confirm if your location is covered.
Does the school provide meals?
No. Christ Nation Group of Schools does not operate a boarding system, so meals are not provided as part of the regular school programme. However, upon request from parents, provisions can be made for healthy and nutritious meals and snacks for students.

How can parents communicate with teachers?
Parents can communicate with teachers through:

  • The parent portal for academic updates
  • School events and open days
We encourage open communication between home and school to support each child's success.
