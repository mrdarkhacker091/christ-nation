The Story of Christ Nation Group of Schools

The Beginning — 2017

Every great story begins with a vision. Ours began on September 18, 2017. On that day, Christ Nation Group of Schools opened its doors with a bold dream: to raise a generation that would constantly contend for excellence.

What started as a single campus has grown to four across Ogun State, serving over 1,000 students from Nursery to College. Our WAEC/NECO results and university placements are proof that this vision works. But the real measure is in the leaders we're raising.

The Beginning — 2017

Our Journey

Key milestones in the history of Christ Nation Group of Schools.

2017 – Founding
2017

The Beginning

September 18, 2017. Christ Nation Group of Schools opened its doors with a bold dream: to raise a generation that would constantly contend for excellence. The school started with a small group of students and a passionate team of educators committed to raising leaders through excellence, character, and faith.

2018 – First Expansion
2018

First Expansion

By the end of our first academic session, something remarkable had happened. Our classrooms were filling up, and the demand for admission continued to grow. Parents had found what they were looking for: a school committed to investing in their children's future. We had begun to outgrow our initial facilities faster than expected, demonstrating the growing demand for an education built on excellence, character, and faith.

2020 – First WAEC/NECO
2020

First External Examination Cohort

Christ Nation Group of Schools reached a major academic milestone as our first set of students sat for the WAEC and NECO external examinations, achieving an outstanding 95% pass rate. This achievement was a defining moment in our journey, demonstrating the strength of the academic foundation we had built and marking Christ Nation's successful entry into external examinations.

2021 – STEAM Innovation
2021

STEAM Innovation

Christ Nation launched its STEAM Exhibitions, bringing together Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics. The initiative gave students the opportunity to turn classroom knowledge into practical projects and innovations. Students have explored and created projects such as an Automated Teller Machine (ATM), hydraulic pumps, electricity generation models, rockets, and many other innovative creations. Through STEAM, our students are encouraged to think creatively, solve problems, develop practical skills, and see that they can turn ideas into reality.

2025 – Four Campuses
2025

Four Campuses, Expanding Impact

Christ Nation Group of Schools had grown to four campuses across Ogun State, comprising Christ Nation Nursery & Primary School in Laderin and Asipa, as well as Christ Nation College in Laderin and Agbajege. The school continued to serve a growing number of students and families, expanding its impact while remaining committed to its vision of educational excellence.

That same year, Christ Nation was recognised by the Mathematics Association of Nigeria, Ogun State Chapter, as one of the top five schools in Mathematics. This achievement reflected our commitment to strengthening mathematics education through initiatives such as Abacus training.

2026 – Published Authors
2026

From Students to Published Authors

Christ Nation opened an exciting new chapter by giving young writers a platform to become published authors. Some of the books written by our Junior Secondary School students include The Pink Tiger, John the Disobedient Boy, among others. Through this initiative, our students are discovering that they are not just preparing for the future; they can begin creating, inspiring, and shaping it today.

2026 – Present
2026

Continuing the Legacy

Today, Christ Nation Group of Schools continues to grow with four campuses and over 1,000 students. No matter how much the world evolves, one thing remains true: a poor educational foundation can derail an entire future. That is why we remain committed to giving every child a strong foundation in academics, character, innovation, and faith. The world will change. The future will evolve. But a strong foundation will always matter.

EXCELLENCE IS THE BREATH WE BREATHE.

Our Founder

The visionary behind Christ Nation Group of Schools.

Dr. Timmy Wigwe

Dr. Timmy Wigwe

Executive Director, Christ Nation Group of Schools

Dr. Timmy Wigwe is the visionary founder and Executive Director of Christ Nation Group of Schools. With over 20 years of experience in education, Dr. Timmy Wigwe is passionate about raising a generation of leaders through excellence, character, and faith.

Dr. Timmy Wigwe's educational philosophy is rooted in the belief that every child is uniquely gifted and deserves an education that develops them intellectually, socially, and spiritually. Under his leadership, Christ Nation has grown from a single campus to four thriving campuses across Ogun State.

His commitment to holistic education has earned Christ Nation a reputation for academic excellence, character development, and a strong sense of community. Dr. Timmy Wigwe continues to inspire students, teachers, and parents alike with his vision and dedication.
