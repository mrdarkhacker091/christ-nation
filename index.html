Our History

Christ Nation Group of Schools — established 2017, dedicated to raising leaders through excellence, character, and faith.

Founded with a vision to provide holistic education that nurtures the whole child, Christ Nation has grown to four campuses across Ogun State. Our journey has been guided by the belief that every child is uniquely gifted and deserves an education that develops them intellectually, socially, and spiritually.

Today, we serve over 500 students from Nursery through to Secondary and Vocational Training. Our consistent WAEC/NECO performance and university placements speak to our commitment to academic excellence.

Our Programs

From early years through to vocational training, we nurture every stage of your child's development.

Nursery

Our Nursery programme provides a safe, nurturing environment where your child explores, learns through play, and develops social skills. We focus on building independence, language, and motor skills.

Primary

Our Primary curriculum covers the Nigerian national curriculum with enrichment in literacy, numeracy, science, and creative arts. We foster a love for learning, critical thinking, and moral values.

Secondary

Our secondary programme prepares students for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB examinations. With a focus on academic excellence, leadership, and character development.

Vocational Training

For students who wish to pursue practical skills, we offer vocational training in ICT, catering, fashion design, and more. This programme equips students with employable skills and entrepreneurial mindset.

Co-Curricular Activities

Our students participate in sports, music, drama, debates, and clubs. These activities build confidence, teamwork, and creativity, ensuring a well‑rounded education.

Leadership & Character

Through our Student Government and engaging student gatherings, we provide opportunities for students to develop leadership, confidence, character, and the ability to learn, ask questions, and engage with important issues beyond the classroom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our school.

What are the school fees?

Fee structure varies by campus and programme. Please contact our office for a detailed fee schedule. We offer flexible payment plans.

What curriculum do you follow?

We follow the Nigerian national curriculum, enriched with international best practices and a strong emphasis on character education.

How can I apply for admission?

You can start by filling the enquiry form below, then we will guide you through the application process. You may also download the application form from our website.

Is transport available?

We provide safe and reliable school bus services for day students within designated routes. Contact us for details.

What extracurricular activities do you offer?

We offer sports, music, drama, debating, coding, and various clubs. There is something for every child to discover and nurture their talents.

Do you offer Abacus / Mental Mathematics?

Yes! We offer Abacus and Mental Mathematics for Nursery, Primary, and Junior Secondary students, helping them develop strong mental calculation abilities and confidence in mathematics.

Life at Christ Nation

Glimpses of our vibrant school community.

Campus

Campus

Our spacious and secure campus provides a conducive learning environment with lush green areas, modern buildings, and safe play zones. We prioritise safety and comfort, ensuring every child feels at home. Each campus is designed to promote learning, creativity, and physical activity, with well‑maintained grounds and state‑of‑the‑art infrastructure.

Classroom

Classroom

Our classrooms are equipped with smart boards, projectors, and ergonomic furniture to facilitate interactive learning. Each classroom is air‑conditioned and designed to enhance concentration and participation. We maintain a low student‑to‑teacher ratio so every child receives individual attention.

Sports

Sports

We offer a wide range of sports facilities including football pitches, basketball courts, and a swimming pool. Sports are integral to our curriculum, promoting teamwork, discipline, and physical fitness. Our students regularly participate in inter‑school competitions.

Music Program

Music Program

Our Music Program nurtures a lifelong love for music through vocal and instrumental training, choir, and ensemble performances. Students explore rhythm, melody, and musical theory while building confidence and creativity on stage. It is where we celebrate talent, host recitals, and foster a joyful sense of artistic expression.

Library

Library

Our well‑stocked library contains thousands of books, periodicals, and digital resources. It is a quiet haven for reading, research, and homework, encouraging a lifelong love for learning. The library is staffed by professional librarians who guide students in their research.

ICT Lab

ICT Lab

Our ICT lab is equipped with 40 high‑performance laptops, fast WiFi, and a range of educational software. Students learn coding, digital literacy, and research skills, preparing them for the technological world. The lab is used for computer science classes, project work, and online examinations.
