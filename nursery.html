Nursery Programme

Nurturing Curious Minds

A warm, safe, and inspiring environment for children — where every child discovers the joy of learning.

Discover Our Programme

Welcome to Nursery

Help Me to Do It Myself

Your child has a natural drive to be independent which we encourage by creating an environment where everything is just the right size for them to do things on their own. Child‑sized tools that fit their tiny hands allow them to prepare a snack and serve it to friends. A miniature broom empowers them to clean up on their own. They are taught the skills of self‑care and toilet training.

Your child learns a set of concrete life skills and self‑confidence — but, more importantly, develops the belief that they can choose to make a genuine impact on their world. It is a world scaled down to your child's size.

Here, your child paints, waters the garden, sings songs with friends and works with materials that develop fine and gross motor coordination. We believe that learning is natural, and we strive to make the most of this natural quality by enabling children to experience, investigate, care about others, interact, develop, and create.

Our Philosophy

Excellence is a Journey

We believe excellence is not a destination, but a continuous journey of growth, discovery, and improvement. We encourage children to challenge themselves, embrace new experiences, and learn from their mistakes as they grow.

Every child has the capacity to excel. Our role is to nurture that potential by creating an environment where children grow in confidence, develop their abilities, and pursue their passions.

Life in Our Nursery Programme

Glimpses of our vibrant nursery community — learning, playing, and growing together.

Playing with Blocks

Playing with Blocks

Building with blocks develops fine motor skills, spatial awareness, and creativity. Children learn to problem-solve and collaborate as they construct towers, houses, and imaginary worlds together.

Outdoor Play

Outdoor Play

Our outdoor play areas provide children with space to run, climb, and explore. Physical activity develops gross motor skills, coordination, and a love for nature and fresh air.

Sensory Play

Sensory Play

Sensory activities engage all five senses, helping children process information about the world around them. From water play to sand tables, sensory exploration is essential for brain development.

Circle Time

Circle Time

Circle time builds community, language skills, and social confidence. Children gather to sing songs, share stories, and participate in group activities that foster a sense of belonging.

Art & Craft

Art & Craft

Art activities encourage self-expression, creativity, and fine motor development. Children explore colours, textures, and materials as they create their own unique masterpieces.

Story Time

Story Time

Reading aloud fosters language development, imagination, and a lifelong love for books. Our teachers engage children with expressive storytelling, puppets, and interactive discussions.

Practical Life Skills

Practical Life Skills

Children learn to prepare snacks, pour water, set tables, and care for their environment. These activities build independence, concentration, and a sense of responsibility.

Music & Movement

Music & Movement

Music and movement activities develop rhythm, coordination, and self-expression. Children sing, dance, and play instruments, building confidence and a love for the arts.

Independent Play

Independent Play

Children choose activities that interest them, developing concentration, decision-making skills, and self-reliance. Our classroom is designed to encourage exploration and discovery.

How We Care for Your Nursery

Every aspect of our programme is designed to support your child's growth, safety, and happiness.

Warm, Nurturing Environment

Our teachers create a loving, responsive atmosphere where every child feels safe, valued, and supported. We build trusting relationships through consistent, caring interactions.

Independence & Self-Care

We teach children practical life skills — from dressing themselves to preparing snacks — building confidence and self-reliance through guided practice and encouragement.

Language & Communication

We surround children with rich, stimulating conversation to support rapid language development. Teachers model vocabulary, encourage expression, and read aloud daily.

Social & Emotional Growth

Through group activities, peer interactions, and gentle guidance, children learn to share, cooperate, and resolve conflicts. We foster kindness, empathy, and respect.

Physical Development

Daily outdoor play, movement activities, and fine motor exercises help children develop coordination, balance, and physical confidence. We prioritise active, healthy lifestyles.

Child‑Centred Learning

We follow each child's interests and developmental pace. Teachers observe closely and provide the right lesson when each child is ready, allowing learning to unfold naturally.

Why Choose Our Nursery Programme?

Here's why parents trust us with their little ones.

Small Classes

Low teacher-to-child ratio ensures individual attention and support for every child's unique needs.

Qualified Teachers

Our teachers are certified early childhood professionals with training in nursery development and care.

Safe & Nurturing

Our classrooms are designed with safety in mind, with child-sized furniture, secure outdoor spaces, and constant supervision.

Holistic Development

We nurture cognitive, social, emotional, physical, and spiritual development through a balanced programme.

Parent Partnership

We communicate regularly with parents about their child's progress, milestones, and daily experiences.

Faith & Character

We introduce children to God's love through Bible stories, songs, and prayers in a warm, age‑appropriate way.
