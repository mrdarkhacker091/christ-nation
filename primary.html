Primary Programme

Building Strong Foundations

A vibrant, child‑centred learning environment for children — where curiosity is celebrated and every child discovers their unique potential.

Discover Our Programme

Welcome to Primary

It's a Mixed Age Classroom

Because the curriculum is individualized, your child can work at his or her own pace while participating in a mixed age classroom community. Your younger child learns by observing the behaviour and activities of the older children. Your older child gains self‑confidence, leadership skills and responsibility by setting an example for their younger friends.

Our Primary programme is designed to help your child become their best self. Through "grace and courtesy" activities, we teach your child how to solve conflicts, how to act politely in social situations and how to be kind and helpful to friends.

A cornerstone of our programme is our belief in children's natural creativity. We know children get great pleasure from being involved in the arts, and so we encourage this at every age. Children learn skills and gain immense self‑esteem when given diverse opportunities to create. Art is an integral part of our programme, and we provide a wide array of materials for creating.

Excellence is a Journey

We believe excellence is not a destination, but a continuous journey of growth, discovery, and improvement. We encourage children to challenge themselves, embrace new experiences, and learn from their mistakes as they grow.

Every child has the capacity to excel. Our role is to nurture that potential by creating an environment where children grow in confidence, develop their abilities, and pursue their passions.

Life in Our Primary Programme

Glimpses of our vibrant primary community — learning, exploring, and growing together.

Project Work

Project Work

Children engage in hands‑on projects that integrate multiple subjects — from building models to creating presentations. Project work develops critical thinking, collaboration, and problem‑solving skills while allowing children to explore topics of deep interest.

Science Experiments

Science Experiments

Our science programme encourages curiosity and exploration through hands‑on experiments. Children explore biology, chemistry, and physics in age‑appropriate ways — growing plants, mixing colours, observing insects, and discovering how the world works.

Creative Arts

Creative Arts

Art is an integral part of our programme. Children explore painting, drawing, sculpture, collage, and crafts using a wide array of materials. Artistic expression builds fine motor skills, creativity, and confidence.

Group Activities

Group Activities

While much of the day is spent in self‑directed work, there are also times for group activities. The teacher may gather a group for songs, storytelling, or to explore a cultural, scientific, or historical theme of interest.

Practical Life Skills

Practical Life Skills

Children learn practical life skills such as preparing food, caring for plants, cleaning, and organising. These activities build independence, concentration, order, and a sense of responsibility for the environment.

Science & Projects

Hands‑on learning that sparks curiosity and builds foundational knowledge.

Science Exploration

Learning Through Discovery

Our science programme introduces children to the wonders of the natural world through hands‑on experiments, nature walks, and observation. Children learn about plants, animals, weather, the human body, and basic physical science concepts.

From growing beans in the classroom to observing insect life cycles, every activity is designed to spark curiosity and encourage questioning. We believe that science is not just a subject — it's a way of thinking and exploring the world.

Our Program Features

What makes our Primary programme special.

Mixed Age Classrooms

Children learn from each other in a multi‑age environment. Younger children are inspired by older peers, while older children develop leadership and mentoring skills.

Individualised Learning

Each child works at their own pace, following their interests and developmental readiness. Teachers provide lessons tailored to each child's needs.

Grace & Courtesy

Children learn conflict resolution, polite social behaviour, and kindness. These skills build character and prepare children for life.

Integrated Arts

Art, music, drama, and creative expression are woven throughout the curriculum. Children develop creativity, confidence, and fine motor skills.

Hands‑On Science

Practical experiments, nature study, and observation build scientific thinking and a love for discovery.

Language & Literacy

Rich language experiences — reading, writing, storytelling, and conversation — build strong literacy foundations for lifelong learning.
