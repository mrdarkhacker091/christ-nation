Our Educational Journey

A Continuum of Excellence

At Christ Nation Group of Schools, we offer a seamless educational journey from early years through to vocational training. Each programme is carefully designed to meet the developmental needs of students at every stage, building on the knowledge and skills acquired in previous years.

Our educators and support staff are highly professional and trained. We believe in nurturing the whole child — academically, morally, socially, and spiritually. Whether your child is just beginning their educational journey or preparing for the future, Christ Nation provides a supportive, inspiring environment where every student can thrive.

Our Programmes

Explore our comprehensive range of educational programmes.

Nursery Programme

Our Nursery programme provides a warm, safe, and inspiring environment where children discover the joy of learning. We encourage independence through child‑sized tools and practical life activities. Children learn to do things for themselves, building confidence and self‑esteem.

  • Child‑sized environment designed for independence
  • Practical life skills — cleaning, preparing food, self‑care
  • Rich language development through conversation and reading
  • Sensory exploration and fine/gross motor development
Learn More About Nursery

Primary Programme

Our Primary programme builds strong academic foundations through hands‑on learning, mixed‑age classrooms, and a focus on creativity and character development. Children learn at their own pace while developing essential social and academic skills.

  • Mixed‑age classrooms fostering peer learning and leadership
  • Integrated arts — painting, music, drama, and creative expression
  • Hands‑on science exploration and nature study
  • Grace and courtesy activities for social and emotional growth
Learn More About Primary

Secondary Programme

Our Secondary programme prepares students for academic excellence, leadership, and life. With a broad curriculum and emphasis on critical thinking, students excel in WAEC, NECO, and JAMB examinations while developing essential life skills.

  • Comprehensive WAEC/NECO/JAMB preparation
  • Wide range of subjects — Sciences, Mathematics, Languages, Arts
  • Leadership development through student government and debates
  • Sports, ICT, and extracurricular activities for holistic growth
Learn More About Secondary

Vocational Training

Our Vocational Training programme equips students with practical, hands‑on skills for life, entrepreneurship, and meaningful careers. From cooking and sewing to coding and robotics, students discover their talents and build confidence.

  • Home Economics — cooking, baking, sewing, fashion design
  • Agriculture & Young Farmers Club — sustainable farming, animal husbandry
  • Carpentry & Woodwork — building furniture and household items
  • Coding & Technology — Python, Scratch, HTML/CSS, web development
Learn More About Vocational
