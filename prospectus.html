This prospectus provides comprehensive information about our school, our philosophy, academic programmes, facilities, and the unique opportunities we offer to help every child reach their full potential.

About Us

Christ Nation Group of Schools was established in 2017 with a vision to provide holistic, faith‑based education that develops the whole child — intellectually, socially, and spiritually. Today, we serve over 500 students across four campuses in Ogun State, offering a seamless educational journey from Nursery through to Senior Secondary and Vocational Training.

Our mission is to nurture young minds academically, morally, socially, and spiritually so they can grow into responsible leaders and successful individuals in society. We are passionate about helping children develop strong character, confidence, creativity, communication skills, and a lifelong love for learning.

Our Philosophy

"Excellence is a Journey." We believe excellence is not a destination, but a continuous journey of growth, discovery, and improvement. We encourage children to challenge themselves, embrace new experiences, and learn from their mistakes as they grow.

Every child has the capacity to excel. Our role is to nurture that potential by creating an environment where children grow in confidence, develop their abilities, and pursue their passions.

Our Core Values

Curiosity

We stimulate inquiry and a love for discovery. Our students are encouraged to ask questions, explore, and seek knowledge beyond the textbook.

Imagination

We foster creative thinking in an open, accepting environment. Our students learn to think outside the box and express themselves in innovative ways.

Integrity

We uphold honesty, responsibility, and kindness in all we do. Our students learn to act with moral courage and to stand for what is right.

Independence

We encourage self‑reliance and critical thinking. Our students learn to take ownership of their learning and to solve problems with confidence.

Respect

We treat every individual with dignity and kindness. Our students learn to value themselves, others, and the world around them.

Responsibility

We cultivate a sense of duty and accountability. Our students learn to take responsibility for their actions, their learning, and their community.

Academic Programmes

Nursery Programme

Our Nursery programme provides a warm, safe, and inspiring environment where children discover the joy of learning. We encourage independence through child‑sized tools and practical life activities.

Primary Programme

Our Primary programme builds strong academic foundations through hands‑on learning, mixed‑age classrooms, and a focus on creativity and character development.

Secondary Programme

Our Secondary programme prepares students for academic excellence, leadership, and life. With a broad curriculum and emphasis on critical thinking, students excel in WAEC, NECO, and JAMB examinations.

Vocational Training

Our Vocational Training programme equips students with practical, hands‑on skills for life, entrepreneurship, and meaningful careers — from cooking and sewing to coding and robotics.
