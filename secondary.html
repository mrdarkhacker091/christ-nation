Discover Our Programme

Welcome to Secondary

Learning without Limits

Your child will study both broadly and deeply, covering many subjects that prepare them for higher education and life. Because there is not a rigid schedule or prescribed curriculum that the whole class must follow, your child can focus intensely on their self‑chosen work, with minimal interruption. At the same time, they will collaborate with the teacher to ensure that their work is challenging and purposeful — and that basic standards are met. In that way, the teacher is in charge of the minimum scope of work — your child is in charge of the maximum.

Our Secondary programme is designed to help your child become their best self. Through "grace and courtesy" activities, we teach your child how to solve conflicts, how to act politely in social situations and how to be kind and helpful to friends. We have high expectations for your child, and believe that rewards and punishments appeal to the lowest levels of her intellect. Given a sticker, she will do her best for a few minutes. Given experiences that help her to believe in herself and her abilities, she will do her very best for a lifetime.

Our Philosophy

Excellence is a Journey

We believe excellence is not a destination, but a continuous journey of growth, discovery, and improvement. We encourage students to challenge themselves, embrace new experiences, and learn from their mistakes as they grow.

Every student has the capacity to excel. Our role is to nurture that potential by creating an environment where students grow in confidence, develop their abilities, and pursue their passions.

Science Laboratory

Science Laboratory

Our fully equipped science laboratories allow students to explore biology, chemistry, and physics through hands‑on experiments. Students learn to hypothesise, test, analyse, and draw conclusions — building critical thinking and scientific inquiry skills essential for university studies and careers in STEM fields.

Mathematics

Mathematics & Problem Solving

Our mathematics programme builds strong foundational skills in algebra, geometry, statistics, and calculus. Students learn to think logically, reason abstractly, and apply mathematical concepts to real‑world problems. We emphasise understanding over memorisation, preparing students for WAEC, NECO, and university entrance examinations.

Students develop strong communication skills in English, French, and Yoruba. Our literature programme exposes students to diverse texts — from Nigerian classics to world literature — fostering critical analysis, creative writing, and a lifelong love for reading.

Our ICT programme prepares students for the digital world. Students learn coding, web design, digital literacy, and research skills. The lab is equipped with high‑performance computers, fast WiFi, and a range of educational software, preparing students for technology‑driven careers.

Our sports programme includes football, athletics, and a range of fitness activities. Physical education develops teamwork, discipline, resilience, and healthy lifestyles. Students participate in regular PE classes and external tournaments, building school spirit and camaraderie.

Key Features

What makes our Secondary programme exceptional.

Teacher as Guide

The Teacher is an "Enlightened Generalist"

To quote Bruce Lee: "A teacher is never a giver of 'truth;' he is a guide, a pointer to the truth that the student must discover for himself." We couldn't agree more. Our certified teachers don't have a specific area of expertise and we don't have "specialist" teachers for subjects like art, P.E., or music. Instead, our teachers have a broad knowledge of all subject areas — more than enough to help your child discover interests in any area imaginable and challenge them to deepen the field of study through research.

State of Flow

Achieving the State of "Flow"

Think about how you do your best work. Is it when you are interrupted by others or when you work to an external time table? Probably not. Why, then, is it any different for your child? Our class day consists of long, open ended work periods that respect your child's curiosity and concentration. They may choose to form or join a group to work with concepts introduced in a lesson. And, because they are free to move around the classroom, it's not uncommon for ideas to spread; students are stimulated not just by the lessons they receive, but by each other.

Beyond the Classroom

Learning Occurs Beyond the Classroom

We want the students to be comfortable navigating the world, not just our classroom. So, we have a few excellent books, but not everything there is to read about a topic. As a result, the students must 'go out' beyond the limits of the classroom to find the information or resource that they need. A 'Going Out' is a planned undertaking by a small group of students. They find a resource in the community, schedule the outing, arrange for their own transportation and supervision (by staff or parent volunteers), prepare themselves for the experience and conduct themselves with dignity while out in public.

Resilience

Developing Flexibility, Resilience and Grit

We believe children learn to be adaptable by supporting them to solve their own problems, rather than solving problems for them. With the help of a supportive adult, your child can, most often, find the solution that is best for them. This develops resilience, grit, and the confidence to face challenges head‑on.

Contextual Learning

Learning in Context for Deeper Understanding

Unlike in a conventional programme with a separate time of the day for each subject, your child will gain a much deeper understanding of concepts by learning in context. The starting point for all courses of study is the "Great Lessons" — these impressionistic and scientific stories give your child the "big picture" of astronomy, earth science, geography, physics, biology, history, anthropology, cultural and social studies, language, math, music and art. Meaningful learning happens when students understand the "why" as much as the "what" — and are inspired to learn even more on their own.
