Welcome to Our Virtual Tour

Academic excellence is achieved when students and teachers share a love of learning. Extending beyond the curriculum, we explore each subject in richness and depth. The same dedication is also brought to music, sports, cultural activities and the arts. Our education is self-motivated, rounded and encourages a culture of curiosity and exploration.

We invite you to take a glimpse into the daily life at Christ Nation Group of Schools. From our modern classrooms to our vibrant cultural festivals, every space and activity is designed to nurture excellence, character, and faith.

Campus Highlights

Take a closer look at the spaces and activities where your child will learn, grow, and thrive.

Classroom

Classroom

Our classrooms are equipped with smart boards, projectors, and ergonomic furniture to facilitate interactive learning. Each classroom is air‑conditioned and designed to enhance concentration and participation. We maintain a low student‑to‑teacher ratio so every child receives individual attention. The learning environment is vibrant, colourful, and filled with educational materials that inspire curiosity.

Library

Library

Our well‑stocked library contains thousands of books, periodicals, and digital resources. It is a quiet haven for reading, research, and homework, encouraging a lifelong love for learning. The library is staffed by professional librarians who guide students in their research and recommend age‑appropriate materials. We also have an e‑library section with computers and tablets for digital research.

Computer / ICT Lab

Computer / ICT Lab

Our ICT lab is equipped with 40 high‑performance laptops, fast WiFi, and a range of educational software. Students learn coding, digital literacy, and research skills, preparing them for the technological world. The lab is used for computer science classes, project work, and online examinations. It also serves as a hub for robotics and innovation clubs, where students create and experiment with technology.

Sport Arena

Sport Arena

Our expansive sport arena features a full‑size football pitch, basketball courts, tennis courts, and a multi‑purpose indoor sports hall. We believe physical education is essential for developing teamwork, discipline, and resilience. Students participate in regular PE classes, inter‑house competitions, and external tournaments. The arena also hosts school events and community gatherings, making it the heart of our sporting life.

Assembly Hall

Assembly Hall

The assembly hall is the heart of our school community. Daily assemblies, morning prayers, and special events are held here, fostering a sense of unity and spiritual growth among students. It is where we share announcements, celebrate achievements, and reinforce our core values. The hall is equipped with modern audio‑visual systems to support performances and presentations.

Yoruba Day

Yoruba Day

Yoruba Day is one of our most vibrant cultural celebrations. Students dress in traditional attire, participate in cultural performances, and learn about Yoruba history, language, and customs. The day includes traditional dance competitions, drumming, poetry recitals, and storytelling sessions. It is a beautiful way to connect our students with their heritage and foster pride in their cultural identity.

Culinary Arts

Culinary Arts & Cooking Activities

Our culinary arts programme teaches students practical life skills through cooking and food preparation. Students learn to prepare traditional Nigerian dishes, international cuisines, and healthy meals. The cooking sessions promote teamwork, creativity, and an understanding of nutrition. It's also a wonderful opportunity for students to discover a passion for hospitality and catering careers.

Inter-House Sports

Inter-House Sports

Inter‑House Sports is one of the most anticipated events on our school calendar. Students compete in various athletic events, including track and field, football, basketball, and relay races. The competition fosters school spirit, teamwork, and healthy rivalry. It is also a platform for discovering and nurturing sporting talents. Parents and staff come together to cheer on the students, making it a true community celebration.

Music & Drama

Music & Drama

Our music and drama programme nurtures creativity and self‑expression. Students learn to play instruments, sing in the choir, and participate in dramatic performances. We stage annual productions, talent shows, and cultural performances that showcase the artistic talents of our students. Participation in music and drama builds confidence, public speaking skills, and a lifelong appreciation for the arts.

Science Lab

Science Lab

Our fully equipped science laboratories allow students to explore biology, chemistry, and physics through hands‑on experiments. The labs feature modern equipment, safety apparatus, and dedicated workspaces for individual and group projects. Students develop critical thinking, observation, and analytical skills that prepare them for future studies in the sciences.

School Garden

School Garden & Outdoor Learning

Our school garden is a living classroom where students learn about agriculture, sustainability, and the environment. Children plant, nurture, and harvest vegetables and flowers, gaining practical knowledge about where food comes from. The garden also serves as a peaceful space for outdoor lessons, nature observation, and environmental education.
