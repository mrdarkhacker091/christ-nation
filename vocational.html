Vocational Training

Building Practical Skills

Empowering students with hands‑on vocational skills — from cooking and sewing to coding and carpentry — preparing them for life beyond the classroom.

Discover Our Programmes

Welcome to Vocational Training

Skills for Life, Skills for the Future

At Christ Nation Group of Schools, we believe that education extends beyond textbooks and examinations. Our Vocational Training programme equips students with practical, hands‑on skills that prepare them for life, entrepreneurship, and meaningful careers. Whether it's cooking, sewing, farming, carpentry, or coding — we provide a supportive environment where students discover their talents and build confidence.

Our vocational clubs and training sessions are designed to develop creativity, problem‑solving, teamwork, and a strong work ethic. Students learn by doing, guided by experienced instructors who are passionate about their craft.

We offer a range of programmes under Home Economics, Agriculture, Carpentry, and Technology — ensuring that every student finds an area that ignites their passion and prepares them for success.

Our Philosophy

Learning by Doing

We believe that true learning happens when students are actively engaged in meaningful, hands‑on experiences. Vocational training provides students with the opportunity to apply knowledge, develop practical skills, and discover their passions.

Our approach is rooted in the belief that every student has unique gifts and talents. By offering a diverse range of vocational programmes, we help students uncover their strengths and build the confidence to pursue their dreams.

We also believe that vocational skills are essential for lifelong success. Whether a student pursues higher education or enters the workforce directly, the skills they learn in our vocational programme — teamwork, problem‑solving, creativity, and resilience — will serve them well in any path they choose.

Life in Vocational Training

Glimpses of our vibrant vocational programmes — learning, creating, and building skills.

Cooking & Baking

Cooking & Baking

Students learn the art of cooking and baking — from preparing traditional Nigerian dishes to international cuisines. They develop skills in meal planning, food preparation, kitchen safety, and presentation. This programme builds confidence, creativity, and a lifelong appreciation for good food.

Sewing & Fashion

Sewing & Fashion Design

Our sewing and fashion design programme teaches students the fundamentals of garment construction, pattern making, and textile design. Students learn to use sewing machines, create their own designs, and develop an eye for style and detail. This programme fosters creativity, precision, and entrepreneurial skills.

Farming & Agriculture

Farming & Agriculture

Our agriculture programme teaches students sustainable farming practices — from planting and harvesting to animal husbandry. Students learn about crop rotation, soil health, and food production. This programme instils a love for nature, environmental stewardship, and an understanding of where our food comes from.

Our Vocational Clubs

Join a community of learners who share your passion and build skills together.

JETS Club

Students explore aviation, aerodynamics, and engineering by building model jets and aircraft. The club fosters creativity, teamwork, and an understanding of flight principles.

Vocational Club

A platform for students to explore various trades — from carpentry and painting to pottery and crafts. Students develop practical skills and discover their unique talents.

Young Farmers Club

Students learn sustainable agriculture, crop cultivation, and animal husbandry. The club promotes environmental stewardship, food security, and a love for nature.

Creative Arts & Craft

Students explore various artistic mediums — painting, sculpture, pottery, and textile art. The club encourages self‑expression, creativity, and cultural appreciation.
